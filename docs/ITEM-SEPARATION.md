# Keep belongings apart

In **Packing plan → Keep belongings apart**, choose **Add separation rule**, two distinct checklist entries, and a requirement:

- **Different bags** prevents any copy of the first entry from sharing a bag with any copy of the second.
- **Different reviewed compartments or bags** permits different bags. In the same bag, both copies need distinct recorded compartments; an unrecorded pocket or an ordinary empty area is not substituted.
- **Minimum geometric gap** requires a finite positive distance between recorded occupied shapes in the same bag. Enter millimetres or inches according to Settings. Different bags meet this requirement without comparing unrelated coordinate frames.

Each rule applies to every quantity copy of both entries, in all four approaches. Optional notes record the traveller's reason. One rule is allowed per unordered pair; up to fifty rules can be recorded. There is no automatic inference from an item name, medicine, battery, liquid, dangerous-goods classification, airline booking or destination. Recorded gaps and compartments do not establish hygiene, cushioning, leak prevention or transport/medical compliance. Physically check the walls, containment and belongings.

**Separation checks** appears below the editor, in every expanded candidate, in packing steps and in the offline printable sequence. **Within recorded separation** covers the currently recorded positions of every copy; it does not establish physical safety. **Separation conflict** explains a recorded violation, including saved packed contents. **Separation check incomplete** means some copies, identities or current geometry cannot be assessed; it cannot show a complete result. Required items outside the plan stay listed separately.

The planner checks rules alongside collision, bounds, openings, support, load and weight. It adds candidate positions at recorded clearance boundaries and prefers keeping related copies together where feasible. Bounded order repair can try alternatives when an earlier entry occupies the available bags or compartments. The existing 0.01 mm numerical tolerance applies to the gap check. This is heuristic search, not proof of optimum packing or impossibility. No existing hard constraint is relaxed for separation.

Adding or changing a rule never moves saved packed coordinates or erases completion. Conflicting bags pause new placement; their saved contents may still be inside. Correct the rule or physically review the contents and explicitly undo packed/unlock before moving them. Explicitly removing a rule removes only that preference and allows eligible saved positions to recover.

Removing a linked checklist entry or its reusable library item is blocked until its separation rules are edited or explicitly removed. Separating an untouched quantity copy for a different packing form inherits every linked rule onto the new entry; it cannot silently escape the original requirement. Splitting is refused if the inherited rules would exceed the rule limit. Ordinary quantity changes retain rules for the remaining copies and retain the existing protections for packed copies.

Rules survive ordinary backups, private metadata backups and explicit household sharing. A separate shared working copy remaps the rule and entry identities; publishing strips only that copy's prefix. Invalid, duplicate, self-referencing, orphan, unknown-field or nonfinite records are rejected by import/share validation. Legacy packs with the field absent have no separation rules. Unit changes and untouched imperial edits preserve exact stored millimetres. The optional field is additive; the existing schema version remains supported.
