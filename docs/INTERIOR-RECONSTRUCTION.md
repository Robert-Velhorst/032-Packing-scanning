# Seeded bag-interior reconstruction

The native reconstruction engine and independent web validator represent one connected empty component from a saved Android bag-interior capture. The bag editor now offers explicit opening/seed review, a separate travel-up choice and draft adoption. Saving the bag connects that derivative to collision, downward entry, support, volume, locks, offline backups and packing views. Review alone leaves saved dimensions and plans unchanged.

## Source and reviewed assumptions

The original private PLY is read again, with its existing UUID, target, source hash, finite coordinates, envelope and metadata checks. Reconstruction requires a bag-interior target, at least 300 points, six accepted depth frames, three view directions and fewer than 60,000 points. It uses every saved point, including those beyond the 8,000-point display limit. These are provisional processing gates, not physical coverage certification.

The caller supplies one of six source-coordinate ends as the reviewed opening plane and a finite seed strictly inside the source envelope. No gravity, top direction or seed is inferred. A seed intersecting an observed cell is rejected. Source axes remain distinct from the eventual travel/packing frame.

## Empty-space model

Each source side is divided into at most 46 cells. The target spacing is the same bounded scale as the item reconstructor: the larger of 10 mm and the ceiling of the longest source side divided by 46. Each axis then fits an integral number of cells exactly inside its source bounds. Cell sides may differ slightly between axes; the envelope is not rounded outward to manufacture additional capacity.

Every source point marks its complete cell as unavailable. A six-neighbour traversal starts at the selected empty seed. Reaching any of the other five envelope ends rejects the result as a possible missing wall or floor. Only the explicitly selected opening end may remain open. That reviewed plane caps the returned component's boundary for mesh checking; it is not inserted into the observed-wall mask. A component must contain at least eight empty cells and reach at least four opening cells. Sealed pockets without an entry are rejected. Other disconnected compartments are excluded from this component rather than merged into its capacity.

All observed cells are retained, including clutter and intrusions; none are smoothed, dilated, patched, trimmed or automatically classified as a safe support. The complete sorted observed, free and opening cell lists are returned separately. A free component must have a closed orientable cell boundary with consistent edge winding and one connected fan at every vertex; ambiguous contacts and more than 20,000 boundary faces are rejected. These topology checks concern the sampled geometry.

The [SciPy boundary-connectivity explanation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.ndimage.binary_fill_holes.html) helps explain why a missing wall can connect an interior to the outside. This implementation instead traverses one reviewed empty seed and rejects unreviewed boundary escapes; it does not fill a bag's wall envelope as an occupied item.

## Contract and preservation

The separate native operation is `reconstructInterior`, with capture ID, opening axis/sign and source-coordinate seed in millimetres. It runs on the existing background executor and returns `voxel_cavity_v1`. It does not write or adopt a derivative, edit bag dimensions, update evidence, or alter the source capture. Item reconstruction retains its separate occupied-cell contract and continues to reject bag targets.

The web validator checks source identity/count, exact fitted bounds and cell spacing, seed and opening request, sorted bounded cell lists, disjoint observed/free masks, preview-point membership in the observed mask, and exact opening cells. It independently traverses the observed complement to ensure the result is the complete selected component without an unreviewed escape. It also checks connected topology, outward signed volume and face count. A closed-looking subset with matching volume cannot substitute for the actual seeded component. The returned surface is a free-space boundary, never an occupied item shape.

The hash links the result to the bytes read by the native engine; it is not independent physical attestation. A preview sample cannot independently certify every original point or unseen surface. Both layers reject inconsistent data instead of updating existing records.

## Adoption and packing frame

The saved-source review labels six source ends A-F, provides three sampled seed slices and accessible sliders, and requires confirmation of the reviewed opening and empty seed. The original source is still shown separately in a rotatable view. Reconstruction reads every verified native source point; the slices display only the preview sample and a cross-section of reconstructed free cells. Missing dots are not proof of empty space. Source errors and stale hashes leave recorded dimensions unchanged. Closing the review ignores late results.

Adoption additionally requires a separate source end that faces upward during travel, and confirmation of the lowest floor, new inside corner and recorded restrictions. The opening-up packing frame is a proper axis rotation. It rebases the component at its lowest free floor and smallest free horizontal edges, retaining every free cell. Observed exterior wall thickness remains outside those inside bounds; the complement within the new bounds remains blocked. This can change inside dimensions. The editor labels that change explicitly, and Save applies it; Cancel preserves the saved bag and original capture.

The component is represented by exact disjoint free and unavailable cuboids. Placement checks use actual occupied item geometry against those unavailable cuboids and recorded manual regions. Downward entry checks the swept occupied footprint against both bag restrictions and already placed items. Tops of intrusions are not assumed to support items: support must come from the reviewed lowest floor or a fully supporting item contact. The floor assumption is traveller confirmation, not a detected load-bearing surface.

Upright items point toward the reviewed travel-up end. That end may be sideways in the opening-up packing view; item rotations and written, spoken-step and print instructions use this distinction. Speaking was not exercised. A saved placement retains the interior's source/mask/frame/scale identity. Adoption, removal, rescaling or a travel-up change that invalidates that identity pauses the bag while retaining raw packed coordinates and completed flags. A seed change within the same free component does not itself change geometry.

Support and stacking loads are evaluated separately in the opening-up packing position and the reviewed travel position. Actual exposed occupied-cell contacts rotate into each gravity frame; limits, fragile rules, unknown upper weights, full-path conservative loads and cyclic-path rejection apply independently. An item must have support in both snapshots. The travel base is the opposite closed wall at the inside boundary, not an intrusion top. The virtual opening cap cannot serve as a supporting lid, so opening-underneath travel is rejected.

Adoption and the saved-cavity editor require traveller confirmation of the packing floor, closed travel base, support strength and applicability of recorded stacking limits in both positions. The record binds the source ID/hash, uniform scale and travel end. A changed scale or end clears the draft review; saving a side-up cavity waits for renewed confirmation. Older side-up derivatives without this record remain valid backups but planning pauses until review; raw packed positions and completed flags survive. Re-review works offline without the original native file. Opening-up legacy cavities retain their previous reviewed-floor assumption.

These are two static snapshots. Continuous rotation, arbitrary tilts, impact, pressure, balance, securing and physical strength remain unverified; the review does not certify safe handling.

Uniform calibration uses the longest original source-envelope edge and updates all cavity cell sides and cropped inside dimensions together. An independent size edit removes the cavity only from the draft. Untouched metric/imperial fields retain exact stored precision. Opening dimensions remain separately entered; adoption never manufactures a narrowest physical entry measurement. Manual regions and lid clearance remain recorded in millimetres and must be rechecked against the new corner. Clearance is below the opening in the packing view, not the separately reviewed travel-up end.

JSON backups retain adopted cells, source identity, reviewed travel-up direction, scale, evidence and placement identity; they exclude original native files. Restore validates the derivative before replacing app data and removes native capture links. A restored cavity remains usable offline; reconstructing or reviewing the original scan requires its original Android source.

## Verified behavior and remaining work

The full native suite now passes 40 tests standalone and through Android Gradle. Analytic cases cover all six reviewed ends, exact empty volume, walls and intrusions, missing floor/side cells, wrong entry, seeds on walls, sealed pockets, separated compartments, nonintegral bounds, malformed inputs, repeatability, source preservation and ambiguous edges. Real-file tests read an 8,640-point synthetic open bag beyond the preview limit and leave PLY and metadata bytes unchanged.

The web/server suite passes 221 tests across 26 files. Actual Java save/read/reconstruction output also passes the independent TypeScript validator: 8,640 source points, 8,000 displayed points, 7,820 free cells, 460 opening cells and 2,382 boundary faces. Its estimated cell volume and independent signed mesh volume agree. Changed source hashes, opening direction and forged volume are rejected. No native bridge, camera, phone, emulator or physical bag was exercised.

The adopted-interior domain tests cover all six entry ends, exact rebase/volume, intrusion avoidance in all modes, no support on intrusion tops, blocked downward access beneath roofs, overlap/lid volume subtraction, travel-up rotations, derivative validation and packed-lock conflicts. An actual Java-generated grounded-pillar fixture has 9,096 source points, an 8,000-point preview and 7,708 free cells. Desktop/mobile Chrome checks at 1440×1000 and 375×812 exercise review gates, stale-source/native error recovery, draft adoption/cancel, 48 px end choices, no horizontal overflow, all four modes, step/top/3D/print views, untouched imperial precision, calibration, backup/offline restoration, invalid-backup rejection and late-result cleanup. Original source geometry is unchanged; the browser uses a simulated Capacitor promise boundary, not native/WebView execution.

An earlier opening-up-only Windows desktop Node/Vite check places three, six and nine upright rectangles around that fixture's grounded intrusion in all four modes in 17-90 ms per plan. It independently checks exact-space containment, full support and downward entry for each insertion. This is not a phone benchmark or a worst-case search bound. Search remains bounded and can miss a feasible arrangement; no optimum is claimed.

The added travel-support domain suite checks six signed axes, exact occupied-face contacts, negative base planes, unsupported intrusions, separate load graphs, fragile/limited/unknown travel loads, occupied recesses, cyclic travel contacts and unchanged conflicting packed records in every mode. Desktop/mobile checks cover all saved-cavity travel ends, draft/cancel behavior, source/scale-bound confirmations, stale-review backup rejection, legacy side-up pause/review/resume, renewed calibration review and offline restoration. A separate corrected Node/Vite SSR check places three, six and nine items in all modes in 20-336 ms while asserting each mode's required support fraction in both positions, exact containment, downward entry and no load conflicts. This remains synthetic desktop evidence.

Automatic segmentation, arbitrary opening planes, flexible-wall behavior, hidden intrusions, narrowest physical entry, sensor-inferred gravity alignment and physical scanner/packing acceptance remain unfinished. Finite cells can hide narrow gaps and thicken walls; this engine does not certify real capacity, safe support, closure or fit.
