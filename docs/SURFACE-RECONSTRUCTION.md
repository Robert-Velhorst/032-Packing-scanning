# Saved item surface reconstruction

Android item review reconstructs an estimated block surface from the complete private PLY source. An explicitly adopted, uniformly scaled derivative now controls occupied-cell collisions, intrusions, support, cumulative upper loads and reachable insertion in the planner. Items without an adopted derivative use rectangular bounds. Neither representation proves physical fit.

## Traveller workflow

Open a saved item's editor, choose **Review saved scan**, then **Reconstruct saved surface**. Reconstruction runs locally on the native background queue and sends only the capture UUID to the plugin. The source is read and checked again; the result must match the loaded source's identity, target, SHA-256, point count and envelope. Neither reconstruction nor changing the view edits measurements, calibration, evidence or the original files.

The review switches between original points and reconstructed surface, offers rotation, zoom and reset, and provides a top projection if 3D graphics are unavailable. The cell count, spacing and reconstructed occupied volume are estimates. Volume is not measured capacity or usable bag space. The top projection merges every height and cannot reveal vertical recesses by itself.

Failures preserve the last valid review and all size fields. Closing the review or editor discards late results and disposes the graphics resources. An unadopted reconstruction is temporary. Choose **Use estimated shape in plan**, then **Save changes**, to persist the derivative, original source envelope, exact fitted dimension snapshot and adoption time. This is an explicit use of an estimate, not a claim of physical verification. The original private source remains available for later processing.

The three fitted sides must be a uniform scale of the source envelope. The planner retains cell rounding after scaling, so its collision envelope can exceed the displayed fitted dimensions. Nonuniform edits cannot distort a captured shape into fitting. Dimension edits remove the derivative from the draft; cancel preserves the saved shape. Calibration updates its uniform fitted scale. Backups include the adopted cells and provenance, while excluding original native point files. Restoring removes the native capture link but preserves the independently usable derivative; malformed derivatives reject the entire backup before it overwrites data. Web/iOS can review and plan with that derivative without pretending to have the original Android source.

## Explicit upright review

The adopted shape review labels its six source-coordinate ends A to F. Choose the actual end that must face up and inspect either the recorded frame or the selected end facing upward. Rotate the 3D preview with touch, mouse or keyboard controls; if graphics are unavailable, three complete occupied-cell projections retain the same letters. Projections merge depth and do not verify unseen surfaces.

Choosing a letter alone does not confirm it. **Confirm A must face up** (or the chosen letter), followed by **Save changes**, stores a user-confirmed direction bound to the capture UUID and source hash. Saving a shape with keep upright enabled requires that confirmation; switching keep upright off permits all proper rotations. Cancellation leaves the saved review unchanged. Re-adopting the identical source preserves a matching review; a different source requires a new review. Uniform calibration preserves the direction without claiming the new scale was physically checked.

The direction does not edit source files, measurements or measurement evidence. Backup validation rejects a malformed, stale or falsely sensor-labelled direction before overwriting local data. Steps and printed instructions name the confirmed end. Changing the direction of an already packed shape pauses any incompatible locked pose without moving it or clearing completion. Recheck its recorded stacking limit when changing the top; the app does not infer a new limit or silently refresh its evidence. Traveller confirmation establishes a handling choice, not sensor accuracy, stability or physical fit.

## Bounded representation

`VoxelSolid` projects every verified saved point into the source envelope. Its grid uses `max(10, ceil(longest envelope side / 46))` millimetres per cell, with at most 46 cells on each axis. A separate empty border surrounds the grid. Observed cells block a six-neighbour flood fill from the exterior; unobserved regions unreachable from that exterior become enclosed occupied cells. Exterior-connected concavities stay empty. No dilation, smoothing, component trimming or deliberate hole closing is performed.

This exterior-fill approach corresponds to the boundary-invasion principle described in the [SciPy binary-fill-holes documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.ndimage.binary_fill_holes.html). The implementation is pure Java with a separate TypeScript boundary validator, without a SciPy dependency. That documentation explains the algorithmic principle, not this scanner's physical accuracy.

The result requires at least eight enclosed cells, one face-connected occupied component and a closed orientable boundary: each edge has exactly two oppositely directed incident faces, and each vertex has one connected surface fan. Ambiguous edge or vertex contacts and surfaces exceeding 20,000 boundary faces are rejected rather than truncated. The web validator independently checks cell order, uniqueness, resource bounds, connectivity, topology and signed surface volume against the occupied-cell volume. The surface retains exterior concavities rather than substituting a filled rectangular envelope.

The native read also requires the existing capture minimums of 300 source points, six accepted depth frames and three view directions, and rejects captures reaching the 60,000-point cap. These thresholds are provisional processing gates, not proof of sufficient scan coverage. Original previews remain limited to 8,000 display points; reconstruction uses all saved points.

## Packing integration

The planner groups occupied cells into disjoint exact cuboids without trimming cells or filling recesses. A cached boundary mesh draws the same geometry in the 3D plan; occupied projections appear in step and print diagrams. Occupied volume counts cells, while other items retain rectangular volumes. Twenty-four proper axis rotations preserve handedness. With keep upright enabled, a confirmed source end must point upward; four proper rotations around that direction remain available. Older derivatives without a review retain the stored positive height axis and show a review warning. The scan axes do not automatically identify the real top.

Collision checks compare occupied cuboids against other items and recorded unavailable regions. The bag's usable bounds, lid clearance and opening stay separate. Opening checks retain the conservative rotated rectangular envelope; angled insertion is not simulated. Candidates require straight downward entry in their final orientation. A swept occupied volume rejects geometrically free positions trapped beneath a roof. The displayed insertion order preserves the solver's tested order, including cases where a smaller object must enter before a surrounding shape.

Support checks use occupied bottom patches at the item's lowest plane and the lower item's exposed horizontal surfaces. They do not assign support to a rectangular envelope over a void. Cumulative loads follow the same contacts and retain the existing full-weight accounting on every support path, fragile no-stack rules and sourced additional-weight limits. Support fractions do not verify stability, pressure, securing or internal strength of an irregular object. Load transfer also considers every exposed downward face, including an interior underside touching another item when both stand on the floor. Mutual interlocking contacts are rejected as cyclic load paths instead of inventing a load-sharing solution.

Contact positions include occupied and unavailable boundaries and pending-shape corner anchors. Reservations respect bag/traveller assignment, opening dimensions, remaining upper saved mass and load contacts. The first mode orders use a bounded lookahead at the largest pending shape. If those orders leave items unplaced, the planner tries moving excluded items or groups ahead of eligible enclosing shapes or obstructions. Repair attempts reserve up to three pending shapes with an eight-state beam, corner positions and a small contact sample. Reservations are calculated once per item/bag and contribute actual contact positions to the candidate search, including intermediate recesses. Candidate scores retain room and downward entry for that arrangement. Two- and three-pair fixtures place inner objects before enclosing roofs in all four modes.

The complete search uses at most 24 insertion-order attempts; a remaining exclusion at that limit produces an explicit warning. Each order still checks collision, intrusion, opening, mass, support, stacking and straight downward entry. Saved packed locks are never moved by retries. Contact search remains limited to 20,000 positions per orientation; a failed limited search is disclosed as a search limit, not proof of impossible fit. These limits, sampled reservations and deterministic placement scoring can miss a feasible arrangement. This is a heuristic, not a global optimum or exhaustive nesting search.

Locked placements retain a derivative-and-scale key. Adopting, removing or rescaling a shape does not silently reinterpret a packed placement. Invalid or changed locked geometry pauses new placements in that bag and calls for explicit physical review and unlock/undo; saved positions and completed flags remain unchanged.

## Remaining limits

A closed voxel boundary does not prove the physical object was fully captured. Cell rounding may close a narrow opening or thicken thin features; unseen enclosed cavities are filled. Reflective surfaces, clutter and sensor error can produce incorrect geometry even when topology passes. Sampling spacing does not bound physical error. The traveller can explicitly record a required upright end; this version does not independently verify that choice, infer it from gravity, or establish material compression, cushioning or a safe packing margin.

Bag-interior sources are rejected. Filling an envelope of bag walls as an occupied object cannot establish the free cavity. A separate seeded-interior representation of observed walls, one reviewed opening plane and an empty component now exists. Its explicit opening/seed review, travel-up confirmation, adoption and irregular-container planner integration now pass synthetic and desktop/mobile checks; see [interior reconstruction](INTERIOR-RECONSTRUCTION.md).

Physical scan and packing validation remain required. Physically validated usable-interior reconstruction, automatic and physically validated upright inference, cushioning, stability, stronger search/backtracking, flexible material behavior and arbitrary angled insertion remain open. A successful occupied-cell placement remains a suggestion.

## Verified behavior

The current web/server suite passes 186 tests. The current complete native gate passes 40 geometry/storage tests, both standalone and through Android Gradle, including the separate seeded-interior engine. Item reconstruction behavior remains covered; see [interior implementation and integration work](INTERIOR-RECONSTRUCTION.md). Synthetic closed shells verify exact occupied volume, outward faces, repeatability and source preservation. A U-shaped shell preserves its open concavity. Open shells, disconnected objects, malformed bounds, oversized sources and ambiguous edge contacts are rejected. Real-file tests reconstruct sources beyond the preview limit while leaving PLY and metadata bytes unchanged.

Desktop/mobile Chrome checks at 1440×1000 and 375×812 use actual Java save/read/reconstruction DTOs for a cube and a U-shaped shell with a simulated Capacitor promise boundary. They check explicit reconstruction, switching, rotation, projection, unchanged measurements and storage, rejected hashes/cells, open-shell errors, bag exclusion, graphics loss, offline reload and late-result cleanup. Neither these checks nor the Android build establish native/WebView transport on a phone, camera accuracy or physical fit. See the [dated build evidence](QA-ANDROID-BUILD.md).

Adopted-shape browser checks also cover explicit saving, rejection of nonuniform scaling, all four modes packing inside a U recess, exact unit/provenance preservation, occupied-cell step and print diagrams, derivative backup/restore without native-source links, malformed derivative and locked-position rejection, and locked-geometry recovery. The two-page sample PDF was rendered and visually reviewed. A desktop Node/Vite benchmark with 2, 6 and 10 mixed U-shaped and rectangular instances completed in 4–137 ms per mode, with all instances placed and no recorded-load conflicts; this does not establish phone or physical-packing performance.

The later insertion-order checks add actual Java-derived synthetic arches with two independent inner/outer pairs. All modes place four items, and each packed confirmation, printed order, backup restore and offline reload retains the checked sequence. The four-page PDF is rendered and visually reviewed. Unit fixtures cover optional inner items preceding required roofs, unchanged packed locks, fragile underside contacts, unrelated traveller/bag reservations, repeatability and disclosure of the 24-order limit. The repeated desktop Node/Vite U-shaped benchmark places all 2, 6 and 10 instances in every mode without recorded-load conflicts in 1–77 ms; that benchmark does not exercise phone hardware or establish the cost of difficult repair cases.

The final reservation-guided checks extend this to three independent pairs: six items in every mode, six successive packed confirmations with a complete remaining plan, a visually reviewed six-page PDF and preserved locks after backup/offline restoration. Confirmation advances according to the replanned order, including when a previously last step becomes an earlier packed lock. Desktop Node/Vite runs with one to three dependent pairs finish in 2–198 ms, with no exclusions or recorded-load conflicts; they do not establish phone performance or a worst-case search bound. The [dated Android build report](QA-ANDROID-BUILD.md) records the final artifact and exact verification scope.

The manual upright checks cover all six ends and four allowed rotations per end, side-captured items that fit only with a reviewed direction, stale/malformed evidence, preserved geometry and calibration, and compatible versus conflicting packed locks. Desktop/mobile Chrome checks exercise all six previews, explicit confirmation and saving, graphics fallback/recovery, all planning modes, named step/print directions, cancellation, backup rejection and offline restore. The two-page upright PDF and both screenshots are visually reviewed. These checks restore an actual Java-derived synthetic U derivative without a native bridge or physical device. Adoption and three-pair insertion regressions also pass on the final bundle.
