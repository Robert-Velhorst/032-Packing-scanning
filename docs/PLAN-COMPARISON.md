# Compare candidate packing plans

On **Packing plan → Fit and trade-offs**, compare Balanced, Maximum Capacity, Easy Access and Fragile Protection before choosing **Use this plan**. The cards use the actual solver candidates rather than sample metrics. Selecting a candidate saves the pack's approach through the existing local-storage path; it preserves recorded packed positions and completed items. Expanding details is read-only.

Each card shows proposed item count, bags used, required items outside the plan, separately marked-unavailable required items, saved weight and evidence reminders. **Bag usage and checks** includes bag-by-bag weight ranges, approximate occupied volume, recorded weight-limit checks and upper-weight margins, preparations, priority-access and fragile-item counts, every exclusion's reason and the candidate's actual warnings.

Weight calculations use the selected candidate's item copies and saved empty-bag weights. Every selected bag's tare is included, including empty selected bags; remove a bag from the pack if it will not travel. Saved item ranges retain both endpoints, including a valid zero lower bound. Repeated copies repeat the range; using a folded or compressed form does not reduce mass. Displayed grams/ounces do not alter stored values.

**Known subtotal** means the full total is incomplete. Missing or invalid item/tare weights, changed/missing preparation forms, conflicting identities, missing bags and unresolved packed positions cannot produce a complete total. Paused or rejected saved positions may still represent belongings physically inside a bag. Unknown masses are not asserted to be zero. A known subtotal that already exceeds a recorded bag limit remains flagged; an incomplete below-limit subtotal does not establish a pass or positive margin.

When changed weight records put saved packed positions over a bag's limit, each candidate shows a separate **Bag paused** notice with the upper saved packed amount and limit. That amount describes the saved physical positions, including positions rejected from the proposed sequence; it is distinct from the candidate's proposed contents. The bag cannot show a within-limit result or positive margin while this conflict remains. Correct the records or explicitly undo packed positions after checking the real contents. See [saved weight conflicts and recovery](BAG-WEIGHTS.md).

**Estimated saved weight** includes estimated, missing, invalid, future-dated or lower-confidence weight sources, and saved weight ranges. A valid non-estimated source with at least 80% recorded confidence can be labelled **Recorded weight**. Source confidence is a traveller's record, not a calibrated measurement uncertainty or success probability. Evidence reminders count selected properties, while weight-review notices count item copies/bags that use uncertain sources.

When candidates have exactly the same placed items, bags, forms, compartments, orientations, coordinates and insertion order, later cards state **Same placed items and proposed positions as …**. Mode labels alone do not imply a different arrangement. Exclusions and warnings remain separate and must still be compared. Empty or infeasible candidates keep required exclusions visible.

Occupied volume uses the solver's recorded usable-space capacity and occupied cells or rectangular bounds. It is approximate and does not establish closure or physical fit. Preparation/access/fragile counts do not establish retrieval ease, safe compression, cushioning or impact protection. Physical balance and closure remain unverified. Carrier booking applicability and source freshness remain in **Carrier rules**; these bag-limit comparisons do not establish carrier acceptance.

The comparisons are recomputed from restored records and work offline after the app assets have been cached. They require no new record format, scan, account or upload. See [the dated verification report](QA-ANDROID-BUILD.md) for the fourteen model tests, rendered desktop/mobile flows and Android package evidence. Actual supported-phone scanning, voice, physical scan/fit/closure trials and iOS compilation remain outstanding.
