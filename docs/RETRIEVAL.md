# Retrieval from a finished pack

Open **Packing plan**, expand a candidate's **Bag usage and checks**, and read **Retrieval from the finished pack**. The same checks appear for the current packing step and in the offline printable sequence. Numbers identify packing steps, including separate copies of the same belonging.

The check evaluates the final proposed contents, not just the items already confirmed packed. **Clear upward path in the model** means that recorded occupied geometry can move straight upward at its planned orientation without crossing another item or recorded fixed space. Rectangular belongings use their full bounds; adopted shapes retain their occupied cells and open recesses. Recorded compartments retain their independently reviewed top access. This does not establish physical access, hand clearance, zipper/lid operation, angled insertion or removal, cushioning, deformation or stability. Open the bag in its packing orientation first; travel orientation is not a retrieval frame.

**Other planned items involved before retrieval** includes direct obstructions, items supported by the target, and their transitive prerequisites. Each distinct copy is counted once. These names are not a safe physical unpacking sequence. Removing a supporting item can disturb another item even when there is room for upward movement. Check actual support before moving anything. Fixed-space obstructions and cyclic dependencies show **Upward retrieval blocked in the model**; an alternative access path is not invented.

Missing, overlapping, duplicated or changed item, shape, form, compartment, opening or interior records make the affected bag's check incomplete. A paused or otherwise unresolved saved packed position prevents clear results for that bag: its contents may still be inside. Raw packed positions and completion are retained. Correct the relevant record or deliberately undo packed/unlock after checking the real contents; eligible checks then recover. Unknown physical belongings outside the saved records cannot be inferred.

Priority access means an entry's existing access priority is four or five. The numerator and denominator cover **placed** priority-access items only. Required and other excluded items stay visible separately; an empty candidate has nothing to assess. No physical success percentage is reported. Every candidate can be inspected without selecting it.

Easy access now ranks completed heuristic attempts using recorded withdrawal prerequisites. A modeled clear path earns the full priority reward; each additional distinct prerequisite reduces that reward. Blocked and incomplete checks earn none. Placement gates, assignments, weight/stacking restrictions, required exclusions and saved locks still apply. This is bounded heuristic planning, not a proof of optimum retrieval or physical fit. The other approaches retain their existing objectives.

Checks are derived from current records, so ordinary backup/restore, unit changes and offline reload recompute them without adding persistent accessibility claims. No schema migration, provider lookup, photo upload or account is required.

Verification uses synthetic records: thirteen geometry/planning tests cover stacks, transitive prerequisites, fixed spaces, independent bags/compartments, open recesses versus roofs, changed/ambiguous records, selected preparation, unresolved physical locks and reward/determinism. These tests do not constitute physical packing observations. Supported-phone capture, voice, real retrieval/fit/closure trials and iOS compilation remain outstanding.

The complete local suite passes **408 tests in 45 files**. Client, server and benchmark-tool type checks and production build pass. Chrome through bundled Playwright checks 1440×1000 and 375×812: final-pack prerequisites/support warnings, current packing step, printable sequence, unit independence, actual backup/restore, offline reload, paused physical contents and correction without changing saved packed positions. Page identity, meaningful content, overlay, console and horizontal-overflow checks pass. Final screenshots and the retrieval section and its continuation in the printable PDF were inspected. Browser plugin unavailable; bundled Playwright used without new dependencies. Evidence is in the workspace outputs: retrieval-tests-final.json, qa-retrieval.json, retrieval-*.png and retrieval-sequence-qa.pdf. Synthetic belongings were used; physical retrieval was not tested.
