# Handling properties and bag-opening evidence

Flexibility, fragility, upright handling and the main bag opening now retain their own source, recorded confidence, review date and note. These records describe the traveller's evidence and judgement. They do not establish physical material strength, scan accuracy, cushioning, rotation safety or fit.

In **My items → Edit**, choose each handling value, then review its separate source and confidence. Sources include known product records, estimates and explicit traveller confirmation. Confidence is entered from 0–100; selecting a source starts at a visible 50 rather than silently declaring certainty. Untouched values stay **Not reviewed · confidence unknown**. Turning on a fragile or upright restriction applies that restriction even if its evidence has not yet been reviewed. Leaving a checkbox unchecked does not confirm that the object is robust or safe to rotate.

In **My bags → Edit bag**, record the narrowest opening independently of the usable inside dimensions, then review **Opening source** and **Opening confidence**. A physical measurement can be recorded here. An adopted scan-cavity opening plane is still a separate reviewed geometric assumption, not proof of the narrowest physical opening.

Editing a reviewed value changes its old confirmation to an estimate with confidence no greater than 50 and a new change note. Changing an adopted interior frame/scale also downgrades existing opening evidence. Review the current value again to give it another source and confidence. Cancel keeps the saved value and evidence. Saving an unchanged record or switching units retains its exact earlier confidence, date and note; saving unrelated changes never confirms missing evidence.

The planner retains all existing fragile/upright restrictions and packed positions. It flags missing evidence, explicit estimates and recorded confidence below 80 for review. That threshold is a product review reminder, not a statistical bound or guarantee. Invalid new evidence prevents new placement of its item, or pauses its bag, while saved records remain available for correction. There is no automatic fragility or compressibility inference and no new compression ratio.

The library, bag view, current packing step and printable sequence display these records. Expanded handling details are included in the printed sequence; opening evidence appears with bag contents. Ordinary backups, optional metadata account backups, explicit household copies and resumable photo drafts retain the new evidence. Old records without the fields remain compatible and unreviewed. No automatic upload, provider lookup or local-state migration is added.

Verification covers synthetic records through the real local editor, storage, planner, printing, backup restore and offline recovery at desktop/mobile browser sizes. Native photo-draft structure validation preserves incomplete fields and rejects malformed evidence. The Android package includes the updated UI and storage path; it has not been installed or exercised on a physical device. These changes advance property-provenance acceptance criterion 3; the full product and physical acceptance remain unfinished.
