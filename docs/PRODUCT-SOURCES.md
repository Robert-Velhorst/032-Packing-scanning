## Product implications applied

- Keep item and container dimensions, weight, and other physical properties separate from their evidence source. A photo does not establish dimensions or mass by itself.
- Let users save packing-only plans without disclosing trip details; make trip-aware suggestions optional and editable.
- Tie the item list to the packing sequence, preserve required items, and explain infeasible placements.
- Provide large packing controls and optional on-device English commands. A confirmed failed placement now produces another rectangular suggestion without moving other locked positions; unavailable-item changes and their recovery are separate. [Packing-control QA](QA-PACKING-CONTROLS.md) records the direct browser checks, automated gates and remaining hardware requirements.
- Damage-prevention research now informs hard no-stack constraints for fragile items and separately sourced cumulative stacking limits. Fragile protection also requires full rectangular footprint support. These are static planning rules; cushioning, pressure, securing and transport impacts still require implementation and physical validation. See [recorded stacking limits](STACKING-LIMITS.md).
- Keep the selected plan and saved records on the device for offline access.
- Traveller-recorded unavailable areas and lid clearance now constrain every rectangular planning mode and appear in the saved offline sequence. This advances the intrusions/closure-space path without claiming automatic scan segmentation or physical closure. [Recorded usable space](CONTAINER-SPACE.md) describes the input evidence, overlapping volume, conflict handling and remaining geometry requirements.
- Android saved-surface review now reads the original private point cloud without replacing edited dimensions. Candidate-oriented envelope estimation avoids the starting phone axes for the checked analytic fixtures, but does not reconstruct a usable bag cavity or establish physical scan accuracy. [Scanner implementation and limitations](ANDROID-SCANNER.md) distinguish synthetic/reference checks from the outstanding device tests.
- Explicit Android item reconstruction now reads the complete saved source and derives a bounded occupied-cell surface. Synthetic U-shaped fixtures retain exterior concavities; open voxel shells and ambiguous topology are rejected. The original source and edited measurements remain unchanged. This advances the reconstruction path, but does not establish physical completeness. Explicitly adopted derivatives now constrain occupied collisions, intrusions, support, upper loads and downward insertion, and are retained in backups and packing views. See [surface reconstruction and remaining integration](SURFACE-RECONSTRUCTION.md).
- Clearly identify the current packing model as estimated occupied cells or rectangular bounds. Native capture retains original source geometry; the adopted collision representation has synthetic verification, while physical validation, real-world closure, deformation and physical fit remain outstanding. The [Android build report](QA-ANDROID-BUILD.md) distinguishes compilation from physical scanner acceptance.

- The current [KLM cabin baggage page](https://www.klm.com/information/baggage/hand-baggage-allowance) gives size examples including handles and wheels, says allowances depend on travel class and ticket type, and tells already-booked travellers to check My Trip for the exact flight allowance.
- The current [KLM checked baggage page](https://www.klm.com/information/baggage/checked-baggage-allowance) gives a 158 cm example measured as length + width + height including handles and wheels, says weight and piece allowances vary by ticket/class/destination, and tells travellers to check My Trip for their exact flight. Reviewed 2026-09-23.
- These pages justify storing a source, review date, route/fare context, and the bags a note applies to. Their general examples are not pre-filled as if they applied to a user's booking. The current app does not retrieve My Trip data or verify booking applicability. It can compare manually entered numeric limits with traveller-entered outside dimensions and saved item-weight totals plus bag tare; this remains a planning check, not a guarantee of carrier acceptance or a substitute for weighing the packed bag.
