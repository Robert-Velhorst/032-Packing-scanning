# Recorded usable compartments

This workflow records up to twelve rectangular packing areas inside one bag. It does not detect pockets from a photo or scan. Each area needs its own narrowest top opening and a traveller review of independent access and supporting closed bases in both packing and travel positions. Only the recorded areas are usable once compartment planning is enabled: include the main area if it should hold items. Space between areas stays unused.

## Record and assign

1. Open **My bags → Edit bag → Usable compartments**. Record each area's name, offset from the bag's inside corner, inside dimensions and narrowest top opening. Choose its dimension source. Measurements use the current display units; unchanged unit conversions preserve saved precision.
2. Optionally record a contents-weight limit and its source, and restrict eligible item categories. This limit counts upper saved item weights only; the parent bag's gross limit also counts its tare and all compartments. An unknown item weight cannot be used within a limited compartment.
3. Check independent top access and supporting bases. Changing an area's geometry, opening, limit or categories clears this draft review. Changing the inside frame through measurement, scanning, calibration or cavity adoption also requires review. Cancel preserves the saved bag. Review is an assumption recorded by the traveller, not certification of support strength.
4. In the pack checklist, choose a bag and optionally a named compartment for each entry. Any eligible bag remains available when no assignment is selected. One entry's assignment applies to all its copies; **Separate one unpacked copy** creates an independent entry for a different assignment. Packed, locked or otherwise recorded copies cannot be silently separated.
5. Follow the named compartment in the step instructions. All coordinates remain relative to the parent bag's inside corner, including raised compartment floors. The top diagram labels areas C1, C2 and so on; the adjacent notes give their names and measurements. Blue outlines show usable compartments in 3D and the printable sequence. On-device spoken instructions include the compartment and position, subject to the existing local voice availability requirements.

## Planning and saved progress

Every planning mode enforces compartment containment, the compartment and parent bag openings, category eligibility, known contents-weight limits, parent gross limits, unavailable areas, lid clearance, actual occupied-shape collisions and straight downward insertion from the bag opening. A raised floor is a separately reviewed supporting base; keep-free intrusion tops remain unsupported. Packing and any adopted travel orientation use the compartment's reviewed closed supporting boundary. Items in different compartments cannot support each other.

Compartment top-access footprints must be separate even at different heights. Overlapping footprints, stacked pockets and side-opening areas are refused because they need a different access model. Estimated bag cavities remain intersected with these areas: recording a rectangle does not turn an unavailable cavity cell into usable volume. Shape envelopes stay within their area, conservatively excluding shapes whose empty envelope extends into another compartment.

Packed positions bind to the recorded compartment identity and geometry/review. A changed assignment, deleted area, different opening, contents/category restriction or review can pause the bag. Raw saved coordinates and packed confirmations remain available. Check the physical packing before undoing packed or unlocking; the app does not silently move packed items. Renaming an area preserves its identity and review. Removing all areas explicitly restores whole-bag planning, while old compartment locks still require review.

Ordinary local/native backups retain areas, assignments and packed progress. Malformed or unreviewed area records are refused without replacing the current workspace. Shared selected-pack records retain compartments; opening a separate shared copy remaps parent bag IDs while preserving bag-local compartment identities. There is no automatic upload.

## Evidence and remaining acceptance

The 2026-10-01 local checkpoint passes 277 web/server tests in 36 files, client/server type checks and the production build. Compartment cases cover all four modes, region openings and parent openings, overhead insertion obstructions, raised floors, category and weight constraints, contents versus gross limits, unavailable-space volume, malformed records, changed assignments, preserved locks, backup and separate shared-copy identities. Travel tests check raised supporting boundaries under both signs of each horizontal travel axis.

Installed Chrome with bundled Playwright checks 1440×1000 and 375×812 against synthetic loopback data. The actual editor, assignment controls, four modes, raised-floor step/diagram, mark-packed flow, precision-preserving unit round trip, cancelled edits, explicit review refusal, changed-opening pause, valid restore, malformed restore refusal, print and offline reload pass. Both generated sequences have four nonblank pages, contain the compartment names and raised coordinates, and were visually inspected. Page identity, meaningful content, framework-overlay absence, empty relevant console/page errors and warnings, interaction results and horizontal-overflow checks pass.

Raw QA scripts, JSON, synthetic backups, PDFs and screenshots stay outside this repo in `outputs/compartments-qa`, `compartments-regression` and `compartments-browser-regression`. Android/native package evidence is recorded separately in [the dated build verification](QA-ANDROID-BUILD.md). No Android camera, permission, provider, voice playback or physical packing acceptance is implied by browser or JVM tests.

The optimizer remains a bounded heuristic, not an optimum or proof of impossibility. Compartment plans use regional contact candidates and bounded order repair; the whole-bag pending-shape reservation/lookahead is not yet extended to regional compartments. Automatic segmentation, stacked/side-opening access, continuous deformation, compression selection, cushioning, zipper/lid mechanics, balance, arbitrary movement and independently validated physical fit remain unfinished. The full product goal remains active.
